Effective Date: February 7, 2026
1. Introduction
CyberHUD ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Zhivoglas, provides system diagnostics, hardware authentication, and remote control features. We value your privacy and prepared this policy to explain how we handle your data.
2. Data Collection and Usage
CyberHUD access and processes the following types of data:
System Information: Hardware specifications (CPU, GPU, RAM, Motherboard), serial numbers, and OS details to provide diagnostics and brand verification.
Process Monitoring: Lists of running processes and their paths to identify security risks and system health.
Media Access: The App can take screenshots and control multimedia solely for the purpose of remote management by the user via a connected mobile device.
Network Data: Local IP addresses and network interface status for connectivity between the PC and the mobile remote.
3. Data Storage and Transfer
Local Processing: All system analysis and data collection are performed locally on your machine.
No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or system data with third-party companies.
User-Initiated Export: Reports generated for AI analysis are only shared with third-party AI services (like OpenAI or Claude) if the user manually copies and pastes or uploads the report.
